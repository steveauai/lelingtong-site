# 乐龄通 隐私政策

更新日期：2026年10月31日

乐龄通由 Triple R Technology PTY LTD 开发。

**我们不收集您的任何个人信息。**

- 本应用没有账号，不需要登录。
- 您保存的行程（起点站、终点站和名称）、语言设置以及最近一次获取的运行信息，只保存在您的手机上，不会发送给我们。
- 本应用没有广告，不做统计分析，不跟踪您的使用情况。
- 为了获取运行信息，本应用会联网访问新州交通局（Transport for NSW）的开放数据接口。这个请求不包含您的行程或任何个人信息。新州交通局如何处理网络请求，以其隐私政策为准。
- 删除本应用，手机上保存的所有数据也会一起删除。

如有疑问，请发邮件至：support@iweave.com.au

# LelingTong privacy policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

LelingTong is developed by Triple R Technology PTY LTD.

**We do not collect any personal information.**

- The app has no accounts and needs no sign-in.
- Your saved trips (stations and names), your language setting and the most recently fetched service information are stored only on your phone and are never sent to us.
- The app has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
- To get service information, the app connects to the Transport for NSW open data service. That request contains none of your trips or personal information. Transport for NSW's handling of network requests is covered by its own privacy policy.
- Deleting the app deletes all of its data from your phone.

Questions: support@iweave.com.au
